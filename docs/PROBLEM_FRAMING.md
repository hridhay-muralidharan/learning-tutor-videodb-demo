# EkStep: problem framing

This is the current framing of the problem. The wording is intentionally close to the originating thought. The synthesis is in the order of the argument, not in replacing its language.

## It starts with me personally

I spent 5 years in college learning chemistry and electronics and instrumentation and now pursue product management as a profession which has no relevance to what i studied in college. i am still upskilling as a product manager. now i don’t even remember those subjects i studied in college. further, many of the general subjects that i learnt in school are forgotten and i am learning about the important life skills and knowledge from social media (for example, how to read a label on a packaged food item).

This problem is not limited just to me. Infact, there are many tech professionals who studied different subjects in college that have no relevance to their current profession.

But the segment that i belong to are those students who graduated from tier 1 colleges.

There are larger segments of students who graduate from tier 2/3 colleges and who do not possess the skills needed to actually succeed on the job.

## The problem

I am taught theory in school, not focused real world outcomes. I am taught physics, chemistry, biology. Not astronomy, energy, medicine etc. The distinction is subtle but significant. The subject matter remains the same, but orientation significantly changes.

The chemistry theory teaches us about atoms, molecules, elements, interactions, reactions etc. I am not taught that if I want to contribute to the development of medicines in the world, this is all the theory I must know. I am not taught that if I want to explore the deeper mysteries of the universe, this is all the theory I must know. I am not taught that if I want to heal patients, this is all the theory I must know. If I want to serve public as a civil servant, this is all the theory I must know. If I want to build technology, this is all the theory I must know. If I want to contribute to national or energy security, this is all the theory I must know.

The problem is not necessarily that the theory is wrong. The problem is that its relationship to a real-world outcome is not made visible.

## The +1 system

+1 systems built using Gen AI can be useful in reorienting the same syllabus towards focused real world outcomes and helping children and parents understand much earlier in the journey on the child’s inclinations and then supplement the child only with the subjects that are relevant, instead of forcing a variety of subjects in the name of general knowledge.

The general knowledge that is actually needed only pertains to important life outcomes. For example, the importance of the heart and what happens if certain activities or food items are consumed and how to maintain important heart health etc etc. How the legal system actually works. How to know and defend your rights as a citizen. The actual relevant practical knowledge for living an informed life as a citizen/human in this country.

A person oriented towards public service shouldn’t be expected to memorize the diagram of how a heart looks and works and to label the functionality of each part, only the real world outcome matters.

So, abstraction becomes a key element in defining what is relevant where Gen AI can prove useful. The teachers can prepare a standardised lecture and textbooks that cover the entire theory in depth so the relevant audience can consume the needed theory while for other audiences it is abstracted effectively.

## Evolution of the target segment

Given that there is already an existing education system in state and national level that forces this system on all, the seed/pathway starts from well to do urban indians working in tech professions who have not studied computer science engineering (courses relevant to the job) and recognize that proof of work/outcome beats credentials and are disillusioned with the relevance of the modern education system for their children and actively considering home schooling / already home schooling.

This particular segment will be more open to exploratory education path ways. The results gained from this segment can then be showcased as evidence to progressively more risk averse segments all the way unto gram panchayat.

Once sufficient evidence is gathered across a wide range of segments, scale within a district, across districts. Within a state, across states and finally at national level.

## What still needs evidence

This framing begins with lived experience. The repository must now build the research beneath it rather than turn the experience into a universal claim. In particular, it needs evidence on:

- the relationship between schooling, higher education, and real-world work outcomes;
- employability and job-readiness gaps across tier-1, tier-2, and tier-3 graduates;
- when subject differentiation actually begins in Indian schooling;
- whether early exposure and orientation improve focus without causing premature tracking;
- the size and characteristics of the initial urban parent segment;
- whether evidence from that segment transfers to gram panchayats and public systems.

Until that work is done, these remain the problem framing and the research agenda—not proof that the proposed model works.
