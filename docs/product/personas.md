# Personas

## Independent Learner

### Situation

The learner clones the repo or receives the generated app and wants to study immediately. They may not understand VideoDB, source indexing, course manifests, or static app generation.

### Needs

- Clear first action.
- Honest demo status.
- Small study loops that feel finishable.
- Personalization through plain choices, not configuration files.
- Practice and review without setup complexity.

### Risks

- Confusing demo placeholders for real source-grounded content.
- Getting lost in source panels before starting.
- Treating progress as completion rather than mastery.

## Institutional Student

### Situation

The student uses a course generated by a school, college, coaching center, or training program. They may have assigned curriculum, limited time, weak areas, and varying confidence.

### Needs

- Course map aligned to the official curriculum.
- Next task recommended by progress and review state.
- Adjustable support level without stigma.
- Practice feedback that sends weak topics to review.
- Source evidence where needed, but not raw protected files.

### Risks

- Feeling labeled as weak.
- Being overwhelmed by dense source references.
- Not knowing whether the material is official or AI-generated.

## Teacher Or Instructor

### Situation

The teacher wants students to use approved material and spend more time on weak areas. The teacher cannot personalize every student's study plan manually.

### Needs

- Confidence that lessons map to the approved syllabus.
- Clear evidence rules.
- Ability to set cohort defaults.
- Ability to protect source materials.
- Future path to aggregate weak-area analytics.

### Risks

- Students seeing unsupported explanations.
- App behavior drifting from curriculum.
- Tool becoming another system the teacher must manage manually.

## Institution Admin

### Situation

The admin or academic technology team manages source files, VideoDB credentials, hosting, access policies, and LMS/intranet deployment.

### Needs

- Private source ingestion path.
- Safe generated app output.
- Explicit credit-control gates.
- Documentation for what can and cannot be published.
- Separation between institution-controlled sources and learner-facing artifacts.

### Risks

- Accidentally publishing copyrighted PDFs, private signed URLs, or API keys.
- Spending VideoDB credits unintentionally.
- Losing auditability of what evidence backs each lesson.

## Developer Or Systems Builder

### Situation

The developer wants to customize the generator, templates, source loaders, institution integrations, or personalization rules.

### Needs

- Generic code boundaries.
- Stable data contracts.
- Template-level customization path.
- QA criteria for generated apps.
- Clear separation between product personalization and developer hacking.

### Risks

- Hard-coding demo subject details into generic code.
- Adding generated explanations without evidence.
- Improving local UI while breaking institution deployment assumptions.
