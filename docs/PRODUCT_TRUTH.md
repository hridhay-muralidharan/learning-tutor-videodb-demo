# Pathway learning — product truth

This is the source of truth for the product meaning. It separates evidence, product synthesis, and hypotheses so the repository does not turn a compelling vision into an unsupported claim.

## The product in one sentence

Pathway learning is a source-grounded system that helps institutions connect a learner's foundations and aspirations to relevant knowledge, practice, experience, assessment, and credible outcome gates.

## What the repository can say with confidence

| Statement | Status | Basis | Product implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Education-to-work alignment is weak in India. | Evidence-backed observation | World Bank review and graduate employability evidence. | Pathways must map learning to capabilities and transitions, not only course completion. |
| Degree inflation exists in hiring. | Evidence-backed observation | Harvard Business School and Burning Glass research on job postings. | Demonstrated capability and trusted evidence need a larger role where degrees are not essential. |
| UPSC and NEET are hard gates for their respective destinations. | Structural fact | Limited seats and formal eligibility/selection systems. | A pathway must prepare for the gate without treating it as the only form of contribution. |
| VideoDB can retrieve timestamped moments from approved long-form video. | Prototype evidence | Verified course artifacts and generated app. | Video intelligence can be one reusable pathway building block. |
| A 12–15 year pathway can be evaluated with leading indicators. | Product hypothesis | Indicators can be tested against later examination, work, and life outcomes. | First deployments need longitudinal evaluation and explicit uncertainty. |

## What remains a hypothesis

- Early adopters are most likely to be resource-secure, urban, educated families willing to experiment.
- Meaningful outcomes for early adopters can produce advocacy, capital, and partnerships for public-interest adoption.
- Earlier aspiration clarity and relevant practice improve motivation, retention, mastery, and later outcomes.
- Validated leading indicators can shorten the evaluation cycle for later deployments.
- The model can transfer from urban families to gram panchayats, districts, states, and national platforms without losing quality or inclusion.

## Product boundaries

The system does not predict a child's destiny, guarantee an examination result, replace teachers, or present a narrow aspiration as irreversible. It must preserve foundational learning, choice, re-entry, adjacent pathways, privacy, child safety, and teacher and community agency.
